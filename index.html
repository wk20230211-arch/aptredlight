BUYER DUE DILIGENCE

Evidence matters as much as specifications.

Optical output

Request spectrum, measurement distance, irradiance method, center and average measurements, and coverage.

Safety documentation

Review electrical and optical safety documentation for the exact model, intended use and destination market.

Private label readiness

Confirm labeling, manuals, traceability, packaging, warranty and support terms.

Certifications and regulatory status depend on the exact product and market. This website does not claim blanket FDA, CE, UL or medical-device approval.

QUALITY & TRACEABILITY

Explore our quality verification process.

A serious OEM/ODM decision needs a clear sample-to-shipment review process. Ask for the evidence associated with your exact production configuration.

01

Incoming parts verification

Review LED specifications, power supplies, controllers, materials, traceability and acceptance requirements.

02

Engineering sample approval

Confirm wavelength distribution, controls, mechanical fit, thermal behavior and signed-off reference units.

03

In-process inspection

Agree assembly checkpoints, workmanship criteria, functional tests and records before the production run.

04

Final inspection & documentation

Specify optical test conditions, safety document scope, labeling, packing checks and agreed defect-handling terms.

These stages describe a buyer verification framework, not proof of testing for every model. Request exact-model reports and traceability records.
