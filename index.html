FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Start with the answers
your sourcing team needs.

Which products can I source from APTREDLIGHT?

Explore red light panels, portable devices, LED masks, flexible mats, sleeping bags, wraps, caps and full-body systems. The current catalog is a starting point; request the exact model sheet and available options for your project.

Can you put our brand on the products?

Logo placement, product finish, labels, manuals, accessories and packaging can be discussed for your selected platform. Send your artwork needs and order plan so the team can confirm scope and MOQ.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies with the platform and customization scope. Sample orders, logo customization, special packaging and new tooling may have different requirements. Request a quotation for your exact configuration.

Can we evaluate a sample before placing a bulk order?

Ask for current sample availability, pricing and shipping for your selected model. Agree on the sample configuration and acceptance checklist before evaluating it for a production order.

Which wavelengths and controls can we request?

Use the configuration studio to outline your preferred wavelengths and controls. Selections are development requests; compatibility, component availability and model-specific options must be confirmed with APTREDLIGHT.

How should we compare irradiance figures?

Request the measurement distance, meter, operating mode, measurement positions and units together with the exact product version. Center and average readings should be identified separately.

Which technical documents should we request?

Start with the model specification, user manual, electrical ratings, optical measurements and available safety reports. Request the documents relevant to your exact configuration, intended use and destination before confirming product claims.

What affects production lead time?

The selected platform, component availability, quantity, sample revisions, artwork approval and tooling scope all affect timing. Include your target launch date in the project brief and confirm the schedule in your quotation.

Can we visit the factory in Shenzhen?

Contact APTREDLIGHT to arrange a visit to our Shiyan, Bao’an, Shenzhen location. Share your travel dates, product interests and meeting agenda so the team can confirm the arrangements.

How do we request an OEM/ODM quotation?

Complete the brief below with your product, destination, planned quantity and customization needs. Review the prepared message and send it to APTREDLIGHT through WhatsApp, or contact our official Alibaba store.
