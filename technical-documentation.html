APTREDLIGHT · OEM / ODM SOURCING

Technical documentation for your OEM project

Build your sourcing decision around the exact device you plan to order.

This page explains what to request from our team. It is not a certificate repository or a statement that every report is available for every product. Availability and scope must be confirmed for the selected model.

1. Product configuration sheet

Request the model and revision, wavelength list, LED layout, dimensions, electrical input power, controls, accessories, packaging and manual. Identify any differences between the evaluation sample and the proposed production version.

2. Performance measurement record

Ask for irradiance in mW/cm² with the measurement distance, instrument, operating mode, positions and test conditions. Separate electrical input power from LED nominal ratings and measured optical output. For temperature, noise, flicker or EMF figures, ask for the measurement method and conditions.

3. Model-specific supporting documents

Share your destination market and intended product use. Request available reports and declarations with matching model identifiers, versions and scope. Resolve any gaps before approving the order or making claims on your own product listing.

4. Production and acceptance plan

Agree on the approved sample, configuration change control, inspection criteria, aging-test scope, traceability, packaging checks and pre-shipment review. Put the agreed requirements into the purchase specification.

5. Written commercial terms

Confirm sample and bulk pricing, minimum quantities, development fees, lead time, included accessories, delivery terms, warranty coverage and responsibility for after-sales handling.

Send a focused document request

Include your model, target market, intended use, customization and order quantity. Our team can confirm which materials are available and which items need further evaluation.
