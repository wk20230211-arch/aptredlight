WEBSITE & ENQUIRY PRIVACY

Your enquiry, your choices.

This notice explains how APTREDLIGHT uses the information you provide through connected website enquiry forms. It covers responding to product and OEM/ODM requests; submitting a form does not subscribe you to marketing or place an order.

Notice updated: 16 November 2026.

What happens when you press “Send enquiry”?

Your enquiry is sent to our website service and stored in private enquiry records for our team to review. We show delivery success only after the service confirms receipt. WhatsApp and Alibaba remain separate, optional contact channels.

1. Information collected

InformationPurpose
Your name, business email, company or brand, destination country/market, product, estimated quantity and project messageUnderstand your request, identify the right product discussion and reply to you.
Optional customization requirements, phone/WhatsApp number and target timelineProvide context for the product configuration and follow-up method you choose.

Limited anti-abuse recordsCheck automated submissions and repeated requests. The enquiry service uses a salted IP-derived hash for rate limiting; it does not store a raw IP address in the lead record. Infrastructure providers may separately process connection metadata.

Do not include sensitive personal information, confidential customer data, passwords, payment details or technical drawings in the public form. It does not accept file attachments. Arrange a suitable secure exchange with our team when your project needs one.

2. Contact permission and use

The required enquiry checkbox asks for permission to store the request and contact you to respond. It does not authorize automatic newsletter enrollment, advertising campaigns or automatic messages through WhatsApp. We may use your details to clarify requirements, discuss samples and quotations, and maintain the related business conversation.

Enquiry records are intended for authorized personnel managing the request. They are not exposed through a public enquiry list. We use hosting and storage infrastructure to operate the website and private enquiry records.

3. Attribution and browser storage

The enquiry script keeps bounded first-touch and current campaign labels in browser session storage when available, with an in-memory fallback. It does not store your contact fields or message in session storage or local storage. The fields remain in the open form when delivery fails so that you can review or retry them. Closing or reloading the page may lose unsubmitted content.

Campaign labels can be incomplete, altered by a link, or unavailable. We do not treat them as verified customer identity. We avoid retaining contact-looking values in these labels and do not include enquiry contents, request IDs or UTM values in our confirmed-enquiry analytics event.

4. Analytics choices

Our website uses Google measurement tools to understand page visits and intentional actions. A website enquiry is different from a WhatsApp click, an enquiry confirmed by the server, a sales-qualified opportunity or an order. We do not describe a click as a completed sale.

The enquiry script’s confirmed-receipt event is suppressed when the browser signals Global Privacy Control, the site sets analytics consent to false, or an existing website analytics opt-out is active. The form can still be submitted for a response. This notice does not assert that every analytics tag or third-party service supports every browser preference. Use any available site consent controls and your browser’s privacy settings for broader choices.

Google Analytics may use cookies and process device and visit information according to its configuration. For provider details, see Google’s privacy policy. Do not share contact details in public URLs or campaign tags.

5. Optional WhatsApp and Alibaba contact

We do not automatically send your form to WhatsApp or Alibaba. A manual WhatsApp fallback may open a link containing the enquiry details you choose to send; WhatsApp receives the link information when you open it, and you must press Send to deliver the message. Copying details only places them on your device clipboard; you choose where to paste them.

Alibaba links open our official storefront. Their services, sign-in, orders and privacy handling operate separately under the respective provider’s terms. Review WhatsApp’s privacy policy and the privacy information available through our Alibaba storefront before using those channels.

6. Retention and requests

We retain enquiry information for the time needed to respond and manage the related business discussion, subject to any applicable recordkeeping obligations. We have not committed to a fixed automatic deletion interval on this page. Internal archive status does not by itself mean that data has been deleted.

To ask about access, correction or deletion of an enquiry, contact the APTREDLIGHT team through our existing official WhatsApp contact. Include enough context to identify your enquiry, such as its date and company name; do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents. We may need to verify the request before acting and will explain relevant limitations.

7. Scope and updates

This is a practical website notice, not a claim of legal certification, medical-device approval or universal compliance with every jurisdiction. How a particular business request must be handled depends on its context and applicable requirements. We may update the notice as the enquiry service changes.
