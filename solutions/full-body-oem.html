QUESTIONS BEFORE SAMPLING

Clear scope before commitment.

Are every mode and accessory included in every 3050 ULTRA order?

No. The family presentation is not a contents list. Confirm the exact mat, bag or mat-plus-hood configuration, controller and accessories in the written quotation and approved sample.

Can a professional-space buyer use the same qualification process?

Start with the actual intended use, layout, handling, cleaning, staff responsibilities and documentation requirements. Product-platform presentation alone does not establish suitability or medical authorization.

What is the MOQ, price and production lead time?

Request a written offer for your exact configuration, quantity, packaging and destination. No single MOQ, price or lead time is promised across all platforms.
