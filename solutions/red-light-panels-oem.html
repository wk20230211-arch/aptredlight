QUESTIONS BEFORE SAMPLING

Clear scope before commitment.

Can we differentiate a panel without starting from a new design?

Discuss branding and permitted configuration changes on the selected T300 platform. Availability, engineering effort and commercial scope must be confirmed for the quoted build.

Which performance number should we compare first?

Agree the required spectrum, measurement distance, instrument, operating mode and measurement grid. A headline wattage or one center reading does not describe the complete optical performance.

What is the MOQ, price and production lead time?

Request a written offer for your exact configuration, quantity, packaging and destination. No single MOQ, price or lead time is promised across all platforms.
