QUESTIONS BEFORE SAMPLING

Clear scope before commitment.

Can you confirm one size and specification across every mat?

No single specification is implied. Request the exact dimensions, construction, LED layout, controller and included accessories for the quoted SKU.

How should we evaluate a flexible mat sample?

Agree checks for handling, connector fit, controls, materials, packing and documented optical conditions. Record the accepted sample and any unresolved deviations before a production decision.

What is the MOQ, price and production lead time?

Request a written offer for your exact configuration, quantity, packaging and destination. No single MOQ, price or lead time is promised across all platforms.
