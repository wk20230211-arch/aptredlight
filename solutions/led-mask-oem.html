QUESTIONS BEFORE SAMPLING

Clear scope before commitment.

Are all requested voice commands and languages already available?

No. Share the interface requirements and ask our team to confirm the exact available build, development scope and sample verification plan.

Which documents should a mask buyer request?

Request the configuration sheet, relevant materials and safety documentation, user instructions, labeling and report scope for the exact model, intended use and destination.

What is the MOQ, price and production lead time?

Request a written offer for your exact configuration, quantity, packaging and destination. No single MOQ, price or lead time is promised across all platforms.
