QUESTIONS BEFORE SAMPLING

Clear scope before commitment.

Does every A13 configuration have the same battery or accessories?

No common configuration is assumed here. Request the exact model sheet and contents list for the version quoted; verify the sample before printing packaging.

Can we build a private-label starter range?

Share your target customer, market, proposed product format and estimated quantity. Branding options, packaging scope, sampling and order terms are confirmed in the project proposal.

What is the MOQ, price and production lead time?

Request a written offer for your exact configuration, quantity, packaging and destination. No single MOQ, price or lead time is promised across all platforms.
