Your technical verification checklist

Ask our team to confirm availability and scope for each item. This is a procurement checklist, not a claim that every report is available for every configuration.

  • Manufacturer identity and the production stages performed in-house.
  • Exact wavelength list, LED arrangement and independent channel options.
  • Irradiance measurements with distance, meter and test conditions.
  • Electrical input power listed separately from LED nominal ratings and optical output.
  • Model-specific safety and destination-market documentation.
  • EMF, flicker, operating temperature and noise measurement conditions.
  • Aging and inspection procedures, acceptance criteria and traceable records.
  • Customization limits, change control, packaging and after-sales terms.

OEM/ODM questions

Can I sell APTREDLIGHT products under my own brand?

Private-label cooperation is available. Logo, housing, wavelength, controls and packaging options depend on the platform and development scope. Confirm details with our team before ordering.

What is the MOQ and lead time?

Minimum quantities and lead times depend on the selected product, customization and production schedule. Request a written quotation for your project; there is no single MOQ for every model.

How do I compare different supplier quotations?

Use the same configuration, measured performance conditions, accessories, documents, inspection requirements and delivery terms. Compare the complete specification, not just a headline wattage.
