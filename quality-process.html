OUR BUYER VERIFICATION ROADMAP

From components to repeat supply.

The diagrams and product-family photographs below illustrate what to verify. They are not presented as photographs of factory inspection stations.

PRODUCT FAMILY ILLUSTRATION · NOT A QC PROCESS PHOTO
STEP 01 / 05

Incoming component verification

LED and driver specification checks

Incoming LED, power-supply, controller and housing materials need documented part numbers, supplier declarations, revision control and visual/electrical acceptance criteria.

Evidence to request

Request: approved BOM, incoming check records and LED/driver traceability.

PRODUCT FAMILY ILLUSTRATION · NOT A QC PROCESS PHOTO
STEP 02 / 05

Optical engineering & sample validation

Measured spectra, not marketing wattage

For approved samples, request the optical spectrum, wavelength distribution, measurement distance, meter/method, test date, center and average irradiance, and illuminated coverage.

Evidence to request

Request: sample report with exact model/serial and test setup.

PRODUCT FAMILY ILLUSTRATION · NOT A QC PROCESS PHOTO
STEP 03 / 05

Assembly & in-process checkpoints

Repeatable work instructions and change control

Verify assembly steps, fastening, connector polarity, controller function, electrical wiring and process inspection criteria for the ordered configuration.

Evidence to request

Request: process inspection checklist and approved golden sample.

PRODUCT FAMILY ILLUSTRATION · NOT A QC PROCESS PHOTO
STEP 04 / 05

Final unit safety & reliability review

Model- and market-specific documentation

Request electrical and photobiological safety test scope, functional tests, temperature measurements and appropriate labeling. Certificates only apply to models they explicitly cover.

Evidence to request

Request: matching reports, user manual, label artwork and final QC record.

PRODUCT FAMILY ILLUSTRATION · NOT A QC PROCESS PHOTO
STEP 05 / 05

Packing & repeat-order handover

Protect your brand after first shipment

Approve barcode and carton labeling, protective packaging, batch numbers, inspection conditions, warranty remedy process and engineering-change communication.

Evidence to request

Request: packing specification, inspection agreement and traceability details.
