# OEM / Private Label Buyer's Checklist
## Red Light Therapy Products | APTREDLIGHT
**A practical supplier-evaluation worksheet for US, UK, EU and Australian wellness brands.**
**Purpose:** Use this checklist before approving a red light therapy panel, LED mask, mat or full-body device for sampling or private-label production.

### A. Define the product and target market
- [ ] **01. Intended application.** Specify home wellness, professional wellness, beauty or another intended use. Confirm marketing claims separately from the product's tested and regulatory scope.
- [ ] **02. SKU and destination market.** Record the exact model, configuration, accessories and destination country. Do not assume documents for one version apply to another.
- [ ] **03. Wavelength configuration.** Request nominal peak wavelengths, tolerances and LED count by wavelength. Ask how each LED group is controlled.
- [ ] **04. Optical measurement method.** Obtain a measured spectrum and irradiance (mW/cm²) at stated distances using a named meter/method. Request center and multiple-point average values.
- [ ] **05. Coverage and operating settings.** Confirm illuminated area at treatment distance, treatment modes, dimming, timers, independent red/NIR controls and controller interface.

### B. Safety, compliance and supporting evidence
- [ ] **06. Exact-model safety reports.** Ask for applicable electrical and optical/photobiological safety test reports, including model identifiers, tested configuration and report date.
- [ ] **07. Region-specific conformity.** Review EU/UK/US/Australian labeling, electrical, EMC and other applicable requirements. Confirm who is importer, manufacturer and responsible economic operator.
- [ ] **08. Product labeling and user manual.** Confirm manufacturer/importer identity as applicable, warnings, serial/batch traceability, power ratings, disposal markings and consumer instructions.
- [ ] **09. Claims and intended use.** Align all marketing copy with actual evidence; do not treat registration, testing or a general certificate as blanket medical approval.

### C. Manufacturing and quality control
- [ ] **10. Supplier identity.** Verify manufacturing entity, factory location, legal documents and which production processes are in-house versus outsourced.
- [ ] **11. Approved BOM and key components.** Request LED, driver, power supply, controller, cable and material specifications with traceability requirements.
- [ ] **12. Engineering sample approval.** Agree golden sample, drawing revision, technical acceptance values, sample sign-off owner and engineering change notification.
- [ ] **13. In-process and final inspection.** Define workmanship checks, optical measurement sampling, functionality, temperature/safety checks, AQL method where appropriate and defect handling.
- [ ] **14. Packing and shipment control.** Agree cartons, drop/shipping protection, batch labels, inspection photos and pre-shipment release criteria.

### D. OEM / private-label readiness
- [ ] **15. Branding scope.** Confirm logo placement, colors, housing/strap/fabric, controller skin, manual, label, retail box and master carton artwork.
- [ ] **16. Digital and firmware options.** If APP/Bluetooth is required, request protocol availability, ownership, upgrade support, interface approval and data/security responsibilities.
- [ ] **17. IP and confidentiality.** Confirm ownership of brand artwork, tooling, firmware, custom developments and NDA obligations in writing.
- [ ] **18. Approval workflow.** Obtain artwork proofs, pre-production sample, packaging prototype, manufacturing specification and sign-off milestones.

### E. Commercial terms and growth
- [ ] **19. MOQ and sampling terms.** Confirm minimum order quantity by model, customization type, packaging and destination market; distinguish standard sample availability from production MOQ.
- [ ] **20. Price and Incoterms.** Get a written quotation stating currency, Incoterms, configuration, tooling/NRE, freight and taxes excluded/included, and quotation validity.
- [ ] **21. Lead times and capacity.** Verify sample timing, mass-production timeline, component constraints, inspection time and realistic delivery milestones.
- [ ] **22. Warranty and support.** Agree warranty by model, DOA criteria, repair/replacement process, spare parts, local warehouse availability and response expectations in the contract.
