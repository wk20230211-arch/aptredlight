Red light therapy OEM/ODM sourcing FAQ

Practical answers for brand owners, wholesalers and sourcing teams. Prepared by APTREDLIGHT using the product scope and sourcing workflow published on this website.

What does APTREDLIGHT supply?

APTREDLIGHT presents red light therapy panels, portable and desktop formats, full-body equipment, LED masks, mats and sleeping bags for B2B OEM/ODM sourcing. Its website and official Alibaba showroom provide two inquiry paths. Confirm the exact model and order terms with the supplier.

How should I compare panels, masks and mats?

Start with the intended format and user setting. For panels, compare placement, mounting and documented coverage. For masks, compare fit, contact materials and instructions. For flexible mats and sleeping bags, compare dimensions, cleaning, controller functions and packing. Format alone does not establish optical output or suitability for an intended use.

What is the difference between OEM and ODM sourcing?

OEM commonly refers to manufacturing to an agreed buyer specification; ODM commonly refers to adapting a supplier-developed design. Terminology varies by contract. Specify design ownership, permitted changes, tooling ownership and change-control responsibilities in writing.

What should an OEM quotation request include?

Include your destination market, product format or model, estimated quantity, wavelength requirements, electrical input, packaging artwork, labeling and manual needs, sample expectations and launch schedule. Ask for itemized sample, tooling, packaging, testing and shipping costs where applicable.

How can buyers compare irradiance claims?

Ask each supplier for the measurement distance, instrument, operating mode, measurement grid, center reading and average across the stated area. Numbers collected using different distances or methods are not directly comparable. Request model-specific supporting records.

Are MOQ, lead time and certifications the same for every model?

No universal values are published on this site. Request a written offer for the exact configuration, quantity and destination market. Check that any certificate or test report covers that model and intended use; a supplier-level statement does not establish product approval.
