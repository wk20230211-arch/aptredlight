TOPIC 01

Project scope

01What does APTREDLIGHT manufacture?

APTREDLIGHT is a Shenzhen-based OEM/ODM manufacturer of red light therapy panels, masks and flexible product platforms. We support brand-led product briefs, sample verification and model-specific sourcing discussions for projects in the US, UK, Europe and Australia. Start with a product-family discussion, then confirm the exact SKU, production scope, included accessories and written supply terms. A family photograph or broad company description is not a complete specification.

02What is the difference between private label, OEM and ODM?

Private label commonly adapts an existing platform to a brand. OEM and ODM describe different manufacturing and development arrangements, but terminology varies by agreement. Write down the design inputs, permitted changes, engineering deliverables and ownership responsibilities instead of relying on the label alone.

03Can an established brand evaluate a second supplier?

Yes, you can start a qualification discussion. Define the required product, acceptable differences, test methods, document scope and pilot acceptance criteria. Share only specifications or designs that your brand owns or has permission to disclose, and approve the actual reference sample before a transition.

04What should an initial sourcing brief include?

Include the product format, destination market, intended use and claims, estimated order quantity, desired customization, sample criteria and launch priorities. Separate mandatory requirements from preferred features. This lets both teams identify feasibility gaps and the evidence needed for a useful quotation.

05Does selling into a market mean the product is authorized there?

No. A stated target market describes the commercial discussion, not regulatory authorization. Review the exact model, configuration, intended use, claims and destination requirements with appropriately qualified advisers before launch. Do not infer approval from a flag, product photograph or manufacturer location.

TOPIC 02

Product directions

06Which panel platform should I use as a starting reference?

T300 is a current panel product direction on this website. Use its product page and the public product library as references, then confirm the quoted configuration. Do not treat a development label, an old alias or a related family image as proof that another variant is commercially approved.

07How should I evaluate the A13 portable product family?

Ask for the exact model identifier, dimensions, power or charging arrangement, controller and contents list. Check the actual sample and its instructions. Different family references do not establish that every battery, accessory or feature is included in the quoted A13 configuration.

08What must be confirmed for a voice-control LED mask?

Document the intended commands, languages, user interface, fit, materials and power configuration. Ask which functions are already available in the exact build and which require development. A product name containing voice-control wording is not evidence that every requested language or command has been validated.

09Is the 3050 ULTRA a panel or a flexible full-body product?

3050 ULTRA is presented here as a flexible blanket or bag platform, with mat, bag and mat-plus-hood directions. It is not a standing panel. Confirm the actual variant, controller, dimensions, connectors and included accessories in the quotation and approved sample.

10How should a clinic, gym or professional-space buyer qualify a product?

Start with the actual intended use, placement, cleaning, handling, staff responsibilities and operating environment. Then review the exact model instructions and market documentation. Professional placement by itself does not turn a consumer wellness product into a medically authorized device or establish a treatment protocol.

TOPIC 03

Optical specification

11What does wavelength in nanometers tell a buyer?

Nanometers, written nm, describe wavelength. A wavelength label does not state the complete spectral distribution, irradiance, coverage or suitability for an intended use. Request the exact model and revision's wavelength allocation and measured spectrum, with instrument, calibration status, operating mode, test conditions and date. Confirm record availability rather than treating a configuration illustration as a measurement.

12How should wavelength ratios or channel allocation be specified?

State whether the requirement concerns LED counts, electrical channel settings or measured optical contribution. These are different quantities. Record the selected wavelength groups, controller mode and measurement method, then verify the sample against that agreed definition rather than an unexplained ratio.

13What is irradiance, and which unit should be recorded?

Irradiance describes incident optical power per unit area at a measurement plane. Reports commonly use mW/cm² or W/m². Keep the unit, instrument, distance, operating mode and sampled area together; copying a number without its conditions makes supplier comparisons unreliable.

14Why are a center reading and an area average different?

A center reading is one point; an average depends on the sampling grid and the area included. Request the grid, point spacing, coverage boundary and averaging method. Compare like-for-like results rather than treating a maximum point as the output across the whole illuminated area.

15Can wavelength alone establish a fixed penetration depth or medical benefit?

No universal depth or outcome is assigned by this procurement guide. Effects depend on multiple optical, tissue, exposure and study conditions. Use research only within its actual scope and review the selected product’s intended use and permitted claims; do not convert a wavelength list into a treatment promise.

TOPIC 04

Measurement method

16How should measurement distance be recorded?

State the distance in a clear unit such as cm and identify the reference surfaces: for example, the device output plane and sensor plane. Record alignment and mounting. A distance measured from a housing edge may differ from one measured from the emitting surface.

17Which instrument details should an optical report include?

Request the instrument model, sensor type, calibration information where applicable, measurement range and the method used. Confirm that the method is appropriate for the source and quantity being reported. Instrument readings collected under different assumptions should not be treated as directly interchangeable.

18How do electrical input power, nominal LED ratings and optical output differ?

Electrical input power in W describes electricity drawn under stated conditions. A sum of nominal LED ratings is a component-based figure, while optical output is a measured light quantity. Keep these labels separate; do not present any one of them as the other or as irradiance at a working distance.

19How should pulse, continuous operation and zero-flicker claims be compared?

Record the selected mode, frequency and duty cycle where applicable, with the exact configuration and measurement conditions. Intentional pulsing and unintended temporal light variation are different questions. A zero-flicker claim needs temporal-light measurements or a waveform, instrument details, power-supply and dimming conditions and an acceptance criterion; a phone-camera video does not establish it across every setting. Confirm the exact tested scope before using the claim.

20Can buyers calculate exposure from an irradiance figure?

For a defined constant irradiance at a stated plane, energy per area can be calculated from irradiance multiplied by time, with consistent units. For example, mW/cm² multiplied by seconds and divided by 1000 gives J/cm². This is a measurement relationship, not a recommended treatment dose, session time or efficacy guarantee.

TOPIC 05

Customization and development

21Which customization requests should be separated in a proposal?

List branding, packaging, housing, optical configuration, controls, accessories, firmware and original development as separate work items. Ask the supplier to identify available options, feasibility review, development effort and validation requirements for each. Do not assume that every requested change is an existing production option.

22How should a controller or interface requirement be written?

Describe the required user actions, displayed information, limits, error handling and behavior after power changes. Include the target language and any accessibility needs. Convert a general request such as smart control into testable requirements that can be checked on the exact sample.

23What should a connected-control or firmware discussion cover?

Define connectivity, supported devices, pairing, permissions, offline behavior, update responsibilities and ownership where relevant. Ask which functions are currently available and which need development. Agree the test plan and change notification process rather than treating a concept demonstration as a released feature.

24What matters when changing a flexible mat or bag design?

Review dimensions, materials, seams, LED layout, connectors, cable strain relief, controller compatibility, cleaning and packing. A size or material change may affect several other requirements. Record the revised configuration and repeat the relevant sample checks before accepting it for production.

25How should tooling, artwork and design ownership be handled?

Write down who owns each input and output, who may use or modify it, any tooling charges and the conditions for transfer or retirement. Include third-party intellectual property and confidentiality responsibilities. Resolve these points in the agreement before sharing protected files or authorizing development.

TOPIC 06

Samples and quality

26What does the five-step quality-verification process on this site mean?

It is a proposed project acceptance workflow: components, engineering sample, in-process checks, final review and packing traceability. It identifies evidence a buyer can request and agree. It is not an assertion that every listed test or an independently certified factory procedure applies to every APTREDLIGHT model.

27What is an approved reference or golden sample?

It is the identified sample and configuration that both parties agree to use for comparison. Record its model, revision, serial or other identifier, accessories, instructions and acceptance notes. A sample kept without an approved specification may not resolve a later disagreement about what was ordered.

28What should a sample acceptance checklist cover?

Connect the checks to the actual product: configuration, dimensions, controls, electrical input, documented optical conditions, usability, labels, instructions, packing and accessories. Record results, deviations and decisions. Do not treat receiving a sample or liking its appearance as complete engineering or market approval.

29Is one inspection or defect threshold suitable for every project?

No universal threshold is supplied here. Agree the inspection method, sample size where applicable, defect definitions, acceptance criteria and responsibilities for your product and order. Important functional or safety requirements should not disappear inside an unexplained aggregate quality score.

30How should component changes and repeat orders be controlled?

Agree which changes require advance notification and buyer approval, including relevant components, materials, firmware, labeling and packing. Reference the approved configuration revision on repeat orders. Keep deviation decisions and validation records so a substitution is not silently treated as the same product.

TOPIC 07

Documents and market scope

31Which documents should a buyer request for a quoted model?

Begin with the configuration sheet, instructions, labeling, relevant test reports or declarations, and the commercial scope. Identify the model, revision, intended use and destination market for each request. Ask which records exist and which additional evaluation is needed instead of assuming a complete universal document set.

32What does ISO 13485 establish, and how should buyers verify its scope?

ISO 13485 concerns a quality management system within a defined organizational scope. If a certificate is cited, verify the legal entity, site, activities, issuer, certificate number and validity. Separate company-system records from product-specific test reports and market documents. Certification is not standalone approval of every catalogue product, and this guide does not assert an APTREDLIGHT certificate without matching evidence.

33Does FDA registration or device listing establish approval or clearance?

FDA establishment registration and device listing do not establish approval, clearance or authorization. Ask for the applicable status and supporting evidence for the exact model and intended use. Do not treat a supplier's registration statement, database entry or third-party registration certificate as FDA approval of a product. Check the appropriate product and market documents separately.

34Does a power-supply document cover the complete device?

Not automatically. Match any component documentation to the actual part and its use, then review the requirements for the finished configuration. A document for an adapter or LED component should not be presented as complete-device approval without supporting evidence for that conclusion.

35How should expiry dates and unstated validity be treated?

Check the issue date, any stated expiry, the current model and whether the document remains applicable after changes. This site excludes expired dated files from current public downloads. Where validity is not stated, request clarification; do not describe the document as permanently valid or universally applicable.

TOPIC 08

Quotations and commercial terms

36What is the minimum order quantity for an OEM project?

There is no single MOQ promised for every platform or customization request. Ask for a written offer identifying the model, configuration, quantity, artwork and development scope. Keep sample quantities and production quantities separate so one is not mistaken for a commitment to the other.

37How should sampling and production timing be confirmed?

Separate feasibility, development, artwork approval, material preparation, sampling, testing, production and shipping assumptions. Identify dependencies and the point from which a lead time is measured. Request the current written schedule for your exact project rather than applying an old catalogue estimate to a new configuration.

38How can buyers compare total cost across manufacturers?

Compare the same configuration, quantity, accessories, packaging, validation work and delivery assumptions. Include sample, development, tooling, inspection, freight and support costs where applicable. Missing scope is an unresolved comparison item, not evidence that one supplier is cheaper or offers better value.

39What should the delivery and packing discussion include?

Agree the named delivery terms and place, included packing, carton dimensions, quantity per carton, handling requirements and responsibility for transport or import steps. Verify the applicable meaning of the agreed terms with competent advice. Do not compare quotations that include materially different delivery scope as if they were identical.

40How should warranty and after-sales responsibilities be defined?

Request the model-specific period, covered issues, exclusions, evidence requirements, review process, parts or replacement arrangements and transport responsibilities. Record how defects will be handled and who communicates with the end customer. A generic warranty headline is not a complete support agreement.

TOPIC 09

Brand assets and product presentation

41May I use product-family images as exact-model listing photographs?

Only after confirming that they match the quoted and supplied version and that the intended use is permitted. Family imagery can illustrate a platform, but it may show different controls, accessories or construction. Request an approved exact-SKU photo set before finalizing a retail or marketplace listing.

42What should be agreed before printing packaging or manuals?

Approve the actual model name, contents list, artwork, labels, instructions, intended claims and relevant market information against the accepted configuration. Keep language and revision control consistent. Avoid printing unsupported specifications or copying another model’s document merely because its appearance is similar.

43Can APTREDLIGHT publish a customer name or logo as an OEM reference?

A named reference needs substantiating cooperation records and permission to display it. This guide does not identify undisclosed OEM customers or imply relationships with retail brands. An unfilled case template or a target prospect list must not be presented as an actual project.

44How should Amazon or Shopify teams prepare a product listing?

Use exact-model photographs, a confirmed contents list, approved specifications, instructions and claims appropriate to the product and destination. Check the relevant marketplace requirements separately. A supplier conversation or completed prototype does not establish that a listing is compliant or ready to publish.

45Which records make an anonymous manufacturing case useful?

A real case should identify the product scope, approved requirements, sample or change issue, evidence reviewed and documented outcome without exposing private buyer data. Remove or anonymize sensitive details with permission. Leave missing results blank rather than inventing a customer, performance gain or order volume.

TOPIC 10

Working with APTREDLIGHT

46How do I start a project discussion?

Use the website enquiry form with your company, destination, product direction, quantity and requirements. The connected form displays a receipt only after the server confirms acceptance. Direct WhatsApp and Alibaba links provide separate contact routes; a click alone is not proof that a message was sent or an enquiry was received.

47Where can I find current images, parameters and available files?

Open the public product library, choose a published platform and review its grouped images, parameters and attachments. Fields marked To confirm need exact-model verification. If no file is attached, request it from the team; an empty download section does not prove that a report exists or does not exist privately.

48What if the requested report is not publicly downloadable?

Specify the exact model, required document, intended use and market in your enquiry. Ask whether the record exists, its scope and the appropriate review or transfer process. Do not substitute a different model’s report or infer approval from a document title alone.

49How can a buyer check the manufacturer identity?

Compare the contracting entity, the issuer or supplier information on project documents and the relevant production responsibilities. Request appropriate business documents with the quotation or proforma invoice. Match the legal name and other necessary details to your agreement and supplier qualification requirements.

50What should happen after a useful first discussion?

Agree the next deliverable and decision gate: a configuration proposal, evidence request, feasibility review or sample plan. Assign responsibilities and record unresolved items. A sourcing discussion becomes actionable when the exact product, validation criteria and commercial assumptions are written down, not when every question is given an unsupported promise.
