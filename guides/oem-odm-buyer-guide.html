OEM/ODM BUYER GUIDE

How to choose a red light therapy OEM/ODM manufacturer

An evidence-first sourcing checklist for brands and importers.

Evaluate manufacturers on more than prices, LED counts or brochure specifications. Specify the intended product and market first, then match supplier claims to the exact model being ordered.

1. Define your use case and target market

Describe the form factor: desktop panel, full-body panel, flexible mat or sleeping bag. Include target customer, intended claims, order quantity, shipping destination and app or control requirements. Consumer wellness products and regulated medical devices may have different obligations.

2. Verify optical specifications

Request the measured spectrum, wavelength allocation, center and average irradiance at a defined distance, coverage area, measurement equipment and test method. Distinguish electrical input wattage from optical irradiance. Confirm the actual configuration of each SKU.

3. Verify destination-market safety documentation

Request electrical, EMC, photobiological, chemical and labeling documents applicable to the model and market. FDA establishment registration is not equivalent to FDA clearance or approval; CE marking does not independently verify every product. Use qualified compliance advice for intended claims.

4. Define private-label and quality requirements

Confirm material, finish, housing, firmware and controller customization, branding, packaging, manuals, sample approval, traceability, in-process checks, final inspection, warranty and change control.

5. Compare suppliers consistently

6. Approve samples before scale-up

Test samples against a signed specification and retain reference units. For EU and UK buyers, confirm applicable labeling, importer requirements and safety documentation before printing final artwork.
