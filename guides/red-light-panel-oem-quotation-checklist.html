Red Light Therapy Panel OEM: What to Include in Your RFQ

Sourcing a private-label red light therapy panel from China? Start with a quotation brief that makes every supplier price the same requirements.

For wellness brands in the US, UK, Europe and Australia, the first conversation with APTREDLIGHT can begin with five essentials: target market, product format, preferred configuration, order quantity and launch timeline. You do not need a finished engineering specification to start—but identifying open decisions helps keep the quotation useful.

What should a red light therapy OEM enquiry include?

Compare the complete configuration

A panel quotation becomes easier to compare when the hardware, accessories and service scope are listed separately. Ask whether the price includes a stand, mounting hardware, controller, branded carton, instructions and any agreed testing. Confirm the power-input specification and plug type for the destination.

For optical output, request the measurement distance, instrument, operating mode and measurement area. Keep measured results separate from nominal LED wattage. A product photograph alone cannot establish output or coverage.

From an Alibaba listing to a brand-specific brief

Send the link or screenshot of the APTREDLIGHT product that interests you, then describe the changes your brand needs. Treat the listing as a starting point: request written confirmation of the exact version, included items and available options before approving a sample.

A ready-to-use project brief

We are developing a private-label red light therapy panel for [country / market], sold through [channel]. Our preferred format is [model / size], with [wavelength and control preferences]. We need [logo / color / packaging / accessories]. Please quote [sample quantity] and [initial order quantity], with delivery to [destination]. Our target launch is [date]. Please identify open technical decisions, available model-specific documents, sample cost, MOQ, lead time and quotation exclusions.

Before the first bulk order

  1. Approve a clearly identified sample and written specification.
  2. Record artwork, accessories, packaging and inspection criteria.
  3. Agree how component or configuration changes will be reviewed.
  4. Confirm commercial terms, warranty handling and production timing in writing.

Keep quotation revisions traceable

Assign your RFQ a reference and revision date. When comparing supplier responses, record the quotation number, validity period, currency, quantity tier and the specification revision priced. Ask which version replaces an earlier offer. A lower total is only useful when you can explain what changed.

Separate the base configuration from optional items. For each requested change, ask whether it affects the sample, unit price, one-time development work, packaging or timing. Keep an option marked pending until its feasibility and price are confirmed. An attractive listing photograph is a starting reference, not confirmation that every pictured accessory is included.

Ask what starts each quoted lead time

A launch date, an estimated production window and a delivery estimate describe different events. Ask the supplier to state the starting condition for each interval and identify any approvals or materials still outstanding. Do not turn an estimated date into a confirmed dispatch promise in your own launch plan.

Use an open-item register before approving the quote

For each unresolved item, record the requirement, current supplier response, evidence or decision needed, responsible person and next review date. Useful statuses are confirmed in writing, clarification needed and not included. Silence should remain an open item.

For example, if your brief requests a branded carton but the offer only states standard packaging, ask for a separate packaging scope and cost confirmation. If the quotation names a panel family without the proposed controller revision, request the exact configuration before comparing sample prices. These are illustrative purchasing examples, not reports of actual customer orders.

For a multi-market project, use separate rows for the US, UK, each European destination and Australia. Specify intended use, channel, proposed claims, electrical configuration, supplied accessories, instruction languages and documents requested for review. Do not assume an offer for one destination establishes suitability for another. Keep model-specific evidence review separate from the commercial decision.

Before accepting, check that the final quotation references the approved configuration, includes agreed options and exclusions, and resolves the items that block the next stage. Continue with the sample acceptance worksheet and retain the final offer alongside your configuration and change-control record.

Common purchasing questions

Can I request a quote before choosing every wavelength?

Yes. Send your preferred product format and target market, and identify which configuration choices are still open. Ask for feasible options and their quotation implications.

Does one quotation cover every branded version?

Ask for separate confirmation when the housing, controls, optical configuration, accessories or packaging change. The approved specification should match the version being ordered.
