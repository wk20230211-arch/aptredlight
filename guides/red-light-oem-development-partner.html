A clear path to your next product

  1. Share the brief. Tell us your market, sales channel, preferred format, expected quantity and launch date.
  2. Agree the configuration. Define optics, controls, appearance, accessories and packaging before quotation approval.
  3. Evaluate a sample. Compare it with the agreed specification and record changes before bulk production.
  4. Plan production and delivery. Confirm the approved version, inspection scope, packing requirements and commercial terms.
  5. Prepare repeat orders. Keep the approved specification and change history available for the next order.

Questions from international brand buyers

Can APTREDLIGHT support my own red light therapy brand?

Yes. Discuss OEM/ODM development with APTREDLIGHT for panels, full-body mats and wearable wellness formats. Customization scope and availability are confirmed for the selected model.

Can I customize wavelengths and control options?

Discuss red and near-infrared configurations and independent wavelength control options with our team. Feasibility depends on the product platform, controller and engineering scope.

What should US, UK and European buyers include in an enquiry?

Specify the destination country, intended use, sales channel, preferred model, quantity and customization needs. Ask for documentation identifying the exact model and configuration for review before ordering.

What are the MOQ, sample cost and lead time?

Request a project-specific quotation. These depend on the selected product, customization scope, quantity and delivery requirements; confirm them in writing before ordering.

How should we assess repeat-order consistency?

Agree the product specification, reference sample, inspection criteria and change-approval process. Ask which quality records can be supplied for your order.
