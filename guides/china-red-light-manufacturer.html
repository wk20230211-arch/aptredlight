How to choose a red light therapy manufacturer in China

Choose a manufacturing partner by matching its product platform, evidence and delivery terms to your brand’s requirements.

By APTREDLIGHT · 4 November 2026 · Manufacturer-authored procurement guide

Start with the product you intend to sell

A panel, a flexible mat and a full-body wrap require different materials, assembly methods, packaging and evaluation plans. Define your intended use, destination market, target specification and expected order volume before comparing factories.

Compare six things before choosing a supplier

DecisionAsk the manufacturerWhat to compare
Manufacturing scopeWhich stages are performed in-house? Can we review the relevant production process?Business identity, facility information and responsibility for assembly and inspection.
Product configurationWhat is the exact model and version quoted?Wavelengths, LED arrangement, electrical input power, controls, dimensions and accessories.
Measured performanceHow was output measured?Distance, meter, operating mode, measurement positions and test conditions.
DocumentationWhich documents cover this exact configuration?Model identification, report scope, issuing body and the intended destination market.
CustomizationWhat changes are included and what requires development?Logo, housing, software, packaging, sample approval and ownership of custom work.
Commercial termsWhat is included in the delivered order?MOQ, sample cost, packaging, inspection, lead time, shipping terms and written warranty.

OEM or ODM: choose the scope before the price

For an existing product platform, begin with branding and the available configuration options. For a more substantial design change, agree on feasibility, development fees, milestones and sample acceptance before production. Suppliers use OEM and ODM differently, so record the actual deliverables in writing.
