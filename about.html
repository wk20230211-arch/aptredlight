FACTORY & MANUFACTURING

LED packaging, SMT electronics and assembly — a clearer manufacturing review.

For each project, discuss the applicable factory process and the evidence that can be provided for your exact product. The process descriptions below are buyer review topics, not assertions that every machine or activity is available for every SKU.

LED Packaging & Components

Discuss LED die, package format, wavelength binning, supplier traceability and component test methods. Request relevant photographs and original process documents when needed.

SMT & Electronics

Review PCB revisions, placement, soldering inspection, driver and controller functions, electrical checks and approved BOM requirements.

Assembly & Aging

Agree work instructions, in-process inspection, electrical and optical functional checks, defined burn-in conditions and final packing acceptance criteria.

QUALITY & COMPLIANCE

Make trust reviewable.

Factory photographs and certification logos alone do not prove a specific product is compliant. For the selected product version, request documentation matched to its destination market, exact model and test scope.

  • Optical spectrum, irradiance, meter, distance and test-mode records
  • Electrical, photobiological safety and temperature test scope as applicable
  • Approved sample, BOM, labelling and packaging records
  • Inspection criteria, traceability and support responsibilities

FDA registration is not the same as FDA product approval. CE, FCC, RoHS and quality-system documentation must be assessed for relevance and scope to the exact product.
