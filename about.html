QUALITY & COMPLIANCE

Make trust reviewable.

Factory photographs and certification logos alone do not prove a specific product is compliant. For the selected product version, request documentation matched to its destination market, exact model and test scope.

  • Optical spectrum, irradiance, meter, distance and test-mode records
  • Electrical, photobiological safety and temperature test scope as applicable
  • Approved sample, BOM, labelling and packaging records
  • Inspection criteria, traceability and support responsibilities

FDA registration is not the same as FDA product approval. CE, FCC, RoHS and quality-system documentation must be assessed for relevance and scope to the exact product.
